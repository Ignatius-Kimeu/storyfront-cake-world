Cakes we make

Four kinds of cake, one workshop.

Every style below has come out of Cake World’s Hurlingham kitchen for a real order. Sizes, flavours, and pricing are confirmed on WhatsApp once we know what you need.

Wedding & Tiered Cakes

Two, three, and four-tier cakes for wedding days and milestone celebrations — buttercream, semi-naked, and fully iced finishes, shaped around your colours and flowers.

  • Built to the guest count you tell us
  • Delivered and set up on the day
  • Matching mini cakes for the dessert table, on request

Birthday & Celebration Cakes

From a simple age cake to a full themed build with topper and matching decor — every birthday cake here was made for one specific person and their milestone.

  • Any age, any milestone
  • Names and messages piped in
  • Design built around what the person actually likes

Custom & Novelty Cakes

Have a reference photo, a favourite character, a team crest, or a running joke that needs to become a cake? This is where it happens — edible prints, hand-cut pieces, full scene builds.

  • Edible photo and character prints
  • Team, brand, and workplace-themed builds
  • Bring a reference — we’ll work from it

Cupcakes & Mini Treats

Matching cupcake sets and mini cakes designed to sit alongside a main cake or stand on their own for a smaller gathering.

  • Sold in sets to match your headline cake
  • Good for offices, baby showers, and small parties
  • Same level of decoration detail as the full-size cakes
